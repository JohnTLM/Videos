# Book 4 videos: The Observer and the Taste of Presence

Narrated videos for Book 4 (draft edition), made 2026-10-21 (Andrew; Remotion drawings). They play on the chapter
pages of the Thusness website.

- `build.py` (here, or `tools/series/observer-and-presence/build.py`) runs `tools/build.py` (else the Drive layout's
  `Substrateless-World/Videos/build.py`); settings in `series.json` and `pronunciation.json`; `manifest.csv` lists
  every video with its length.
- One folder per video, `obNN-<slug>/`: `obNN-script.md` (owner-approved), `obNN-narration.txt` (after the
  pronunciation map), `obNN.srt` and `obNN-scenes.tsx` (in the engine: `src/chapters/OBNN.tsx`).
- The MP4s (full size and `obNN-preview-720p.mp4`) are in Drive, `Observer-and-Presence/Videos/obNN-<slug>/`.
- Scripts follow the Anatta `VIDEO-SPEC.md` stance list on top of the base spec. Checks on each video: cue phrases
  against the script (including respelled words), and no empty drawing area for more than 4 seconds.

**Numbering.** Folder numbers are the numbers each video *says aloud*. Most were recorded before the new Chapter 3
"Ignorance and Its Habit", so from Chapter 4 on the spoken number is one lower than in the book today (`ob04-the-given`
is now Chapter 5). The website notes this under each player ("Recorded when this was Chapter N"); the owner chose to
keep the old numbers for now.

Five chapters use a bonus article's video instead of their own, so some numbers are missing here: Chapter 4 (The Logic
of Dependency), 7 (Isn't Energy the Substrate?), 10 (Death and Continuity), 16 (Two Modes, One Seeing) and 17 (Beyond
Cause and Effect).
