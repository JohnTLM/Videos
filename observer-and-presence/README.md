# Book 4 videos: The Observer and the Taste of Presence

Narrated videos for Book 4 (draft edition), made 2026-10-21 in a Claude cloud session with the Drive tools (free
edge-tts voice, Andrew; Remotion drawings). They play on the chapter pages of the Thusness website.

- `build.py` runs the shared builder in `Substrateless-World/Videos/build.py`; settings in `series.json` and
  `pronunciation.json`; `manifest.csv` lists every video with its length.
- One folder per video, `obNN-<slug>/`: `obNN-script.md` (approved by the owner), `obNN-narration.txt` (the spoken
  text after the pronunciation map), `obNN.srt` (subtitles) and `obNN-scenes.tsx` (the drawings; in the engine it
  is `src/chapters/OBNN.tsx`).
- The MP4s (full size and `obNN-preview-720p.mp4`) are too large for this repository. They are in Drive,
  `Observer-and-Presence/Videos/obNN-<slug>/`, beside the same text files.
- Scripts follow the Anatta `VIDEO-SPEC.md` stance list on top of the base spec. Checks run on each video: cue
  phrases against the script (including respelled words) and no empty drawing area for more than 4 seconds.

**Numbering.** The folder numbers are the numbers each video *says aloud*. Most videos were recorded before the new
Chapter 3 "Ignorance and Its Habit" was added, so from Chapter 4 on the spoken number is one lower than the
chapter's number in the book today (`ob04-the-given` is now Chapter 5). The website notes this under each player
("Recorded when this was Chapter N"); the owner chose to keep the old spoken numbers for now.

Five chapters use a bonus article's video instead of their own: Chapter 4 (The Logic of Dependency), 7 (Isn't
Energy the Substrate?), 10 (Death and Continuity), 16 (Two Modes, One Seeing) and 17 (Beyond Cause and Effect).
That is why some numbers are missing here.
