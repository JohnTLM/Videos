## Steering prompt

> Cover all 40 sections of the source in order, with every argument, sutta image and example, and treat every
> "precision note" as binding. Key points: (1) the hinge of the video is that continuity never needed a carrier, not
> even from one night to the next morning; rebirth is no harder to make sense of than waking up; (2) karma and rebirth
> are affirmed, as the tradition teaches them; emptiness does not prove rebirth, it removes only the objection that
> rebirth needs a carrier; say clearly that removing a bad argument against something is not supplying a good argument
> for it; (3) the two extremes, a self that travels on and a self that is wiped out, rest on the same assumption;
> (4) when a carrier is removed, put nothing in its place: no subtler soul, no stream of consciousness, no energy, no
> awareness, not even emptiness; (5) tell Sāti's mistake and the fire named after its fuel; tell Kassapa ("on what does
> this depend?"), the flame carried by the wind, and the fire that went in no direction; (6) present the five schools'
> bridges fairly, then Nāgārjuna's answer: a karma with its own nature could not be made, change or end; emptiness is
> never a power or cause; (7) the clear light at death is the empty, knowing clarity of appearing, never a light or
> awareness beneath experience that travels on; the very subtle mind is not a carrier; (8) tell Dōgen's firewood and
> ash and Baizhang's fox in full: not falling into cause and effect is escape, not obscuring it is clarity; (9) answer
> the brain, memory, fairness and evidence objections calmly, without arguing against science and without endorsing or
> dismissing the reincarnation research; (10) grief is not a mistake; nirvāṇa is neither annihilation nor a place;
> (11) end on the lamp lit from a lamp, "the lamp is burning now", not on "what remains". Pronounce names as in the
> pronunciation list: nah-GAR-joo-nah, chun-drah-KEER-tee, TSONG-kah-pah, nah-gah-SAY-nah, SAH-tee, KUS-sah-pah,
> vuch-chah-GOHT-tah, teh-rah-VAH-dah, DOH-gen, bye-JAHNG, BAR-doh. Main visuals: a lamp burning through the night
> and a second lamp lit from it, a sleeper waking, a house with a traveling spark (to be crossed out), a seed and a rice
> plant, a log fire and a grass fire, a flame carried by the wind, a fire going out with "east? west?" fading, a bridge
> between two banks, a magician's conjured figures, firewood and ash side by side, an old man and a fox, a child and an
> adult, several lamps lit from one.

If the box has a character limit, use this shorter version:

> Cover all 40 sections in order and follow every precision note. Hinge: continuity never needed a carrier, not even
> overnight. Rebirth is affirmed by the tradition; emptiness does not prove it, it removes one objection. No traveler and
> no cut-off; put nothing in the carrier's place. Emptiness is never a cause. The clear light is no light beneath
> things. Tell Sāti, the wind-blown flame, the fire gone out, firewood and ash, and Baizhang's fox. End on the lamp lit
> from a lamp, not on what remains.

## Canon check

The source is the bonus article "One Flame Lights Another", as merged in JohnTLM/EBooks
(`one-flame-lights-another/source/`, PRs #23 and #24, 2026-10-25). It was written from CanonView Part I point 20 and
checked against `canon/canon-view-plain-english.md`, `canon/nature-canonical-view.md` and
`canon/mind-phenomena-and-luminous-clarity.md` on 2026-10-25. The references the article's README lists were checked by
web search on 2026-10-25 (the text sites themselves were blocked); the only verbatim sutta lines are the two already
checked for "Knowing From Somewhere" (MN 4, MN 38). Changes made for the video:

- **Cross-references dropped.** The article's "Part II, §3" style references and its list of Parts are removed or
  replaced with plain wording, and its three "What this Part establishes" summaries are left out, so the video does not
  talk about the article's structure.
- **"Because nothing has a nature of its own, karma works"** is kept, as allowed since canon point 8 was relaxed on
  2026-10-25, and a precision note frames it as Nāgārjuna's reply to his critics, never as a power of emptiness.
- **The clear light and the very subtle mind** carry precision notes (canon Part II points 1–3, Part I point 20), so a
  summary cannot turn either into a ground or a traveler.
- **The reincarnation research** carries a note against inventing case details: the article names Stevenson and Tucker
  but gives no cases.
- **The ending** carries a note against closing on "what remains" or an eternal light.
