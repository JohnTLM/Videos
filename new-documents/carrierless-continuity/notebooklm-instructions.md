   as "Copied text"). Do not add the two earlier videos or their sources: NotebookLM draws on every source in the
   notebook, and the old material would bring the corrected errors back.
2. Choose **Video Overview**, the same Cinematic style as before, and paste the steering prompt below into the
   customisation box.

   precision notes, and the YouTube chapters rebuilt.
