Carrierless Continuity: Identity Without a Soul (Nāgārjuna & Dōgen)

## Description

Your cells turn over, your memories rewrite themselves, your moods never hold still. Yet life feels continuous. For millennia, philosophy and theology have explained this with an unchanging bearer: a soul, an ego, a conserved substrate that carries you from birth to death.

This video argues that the bearer is not only unnecessary but incoherent. Drawing on Nāgārjuna's Middle Way, Nāgasena's lamp, Dōgen's firewood and ash and Tsongkhapa's "mere I", it builds an architecture of continuity with no carrier at all, and shows what follows for anesthesia, death, moral responsibility and the freedom to change.
