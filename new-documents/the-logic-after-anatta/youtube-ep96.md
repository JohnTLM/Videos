## 1. The Logic: After Anatta (overview, ep96, about 15:08)

### Title

The Logic After Anatta: Seeing Comes First, Four Insights and One Koan | After Anatta

### Description

Listen to a car going by. The sound is bright and clear, and it is already passing as it sounds. This moment ceases as it arises. Does it arise, or does it cease?

The overview of The Logic, from After Anatta: notes by John Tan (Thusness) on what turned out to matter after the insight of anatta in 1997. The logic of dependency cannot be understood by reasoning alone: seeing comes first. The four key insights: every appearance is vivid and cannot be found, both seen together; it arises whenever its conditions come together, and the conditions are not a solid network; we habitually think inherently, in the shape of our language; and there is no given world beneath it all. Anatta as the first door, and how the four lead to spontaneous presence. What a phenomenon is: vivid, not found, not even "nowhere", dependent already, and clear with no knower behind it. Why we must first admit that we think inherently, and why bare attention tastes the clarity of appearances but cannot show what they are: the mind is a phenomenon like any other. Six masters on the view: Atiśa, Rongzom, Dōgen, Longchenpa, Tsongkhapa and Mipham. Then the koan read with both logics, the eight steps in brief, the same logic carried to a self, an object and a person, and three things to try.
