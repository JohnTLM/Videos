## 1. The Body's Own Knowing (ep98, 14:15)

### Title

The Body's Own Knowing: Felt Sense, Inner Winds and the Rainbow Body | Thusness

### Description

A dog barks behind you, and your body jumps before you know why. Something in you knew, before you did. Knowing is not only analysis, not only the taking apart of concepts. There is a more immediate knowing, the felt sense of presence in the body, through which fear, clinging, openness and spaciousness are often understood more directly than through thought alone.

To read the rainbow body in a balanced way, neither dismissing nor inflating it, four things have to be sorted out, each with its own video: the energy practices of the traditions (the method shared, the view not), the two fives in Tibet (the elements as qualities, not a frozen stuff), the reports themselves (reports, not proof), and how the body knows. This video is the fourth.

Modern research agrees that much knowing happens before thought; that the felt sense guides change; that knowing one's story and directly feeling the body come apart with practice. The brain predicts the body, so felt knowing is direct but can be wrong. Fear is unlearned through bodily experience; clinging is a lean of the body; the habit of reification is held in the muscles, the diaphragm and the breath, and loosens when the view loosens. The inner-wind traditions, "the mind rides the wind", are maps of this felt movement, not anatomies. Two guards: felt knowing is more immediate, not more fundamental, not a deeper floor; and direct does not mean right. For the rainbow body: what is shown is that the body takes part in knowing and in release; that a corpse turns into light, I hold as a report.

This is not treatment: if you live with trauma, panic or depression, practices like these are a help, not a cure.
