## 1. Inner Winds, Energy Practices, and the Subtle Body (ep97, about 14–15 minutes)

### Title

Inner Winds, Energy Practices, and the Subtle Body: What They Do, What They Show, and Why Emptiness Frees | Thusness

### Description

Channels, winds, inner heat, and clear light across Taoist inner alchemy, Indian yoga, and Tibetan Vajrayana: a comprehensive inquiry into how subtle-body practices work, how modern physiology reads them, where the views part, and why dismantling the inherent framework—not steering the winds—is what liberates.

Energy practices work on the winds; insight practices work on deconstructing the mind's reifications. The method is shared across traditions: posture, breath regulation, an internal axis, heat, and light. But the view is not shared.

This video examines the three subtle-body maps (the Taoist loop, the Indian vertical column, and the Tibetan dissolution at the heart) as practice maps rather than physical anatomy. It clears away modern confusions that turn "energy", "qi", or "rlung" into a metaphysical substrate: the blowing is the wind, and warmth and breath are dependently designated. It details the five root winds and what unfolds when the winds gather at the heart into the four empties and clear light. It outlines why no Buddhist school places energy practices above wisdom, and why classical Taoism (Zhuangzi and Liezi) treats being one with the Dao as supreme over mere longevity breath-craft. Finally, it explains four key departures from the classical schools, why the body still matters (somatic bracing of reification), practical warnings against over-focusing on the crown (srog 'dzin rlung, qigong deviation, Hakuin's Zen sickness), and why luminous clarity is uncontrived, needing neither a pump nor a watchman.
