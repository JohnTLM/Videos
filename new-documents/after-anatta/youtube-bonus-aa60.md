### Description

Who ages and dies? It sounds like the simplest question there is.

The tradition's own answer to who goes round the wheel of the twelve links. A monk asks whose ageing and death it is: not a valid question, the Buddha says, and gives the chain. Is the one who acts the same as the one who meets the result, or someone else? Is suffering made by oneself, by another, by both, by chance? Each time the questioner needs a someone, and each time the Buddha gives the conditions instead. This body is not yours, nor others': it is old karma, felt now. The classical reading lays the links over three lives, in four groups joined at three points, with rebirth and no traveller: a flame lit from a flame. The Abhidharma takes the person apart into momentary factors and finds no self among them, though it keeps the pieces themselves as real. What is kept: responsibility, karma, rebirth and the path. Only the owner is left out.
