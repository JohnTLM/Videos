# The Logic, one full video: cut list (2026-10-27)

For the owner to check before anything is cut. The eight step videos and the overview stay as they are; this list
makes one extra video from them by cutting and joining, with no new recording. Times are in the current videos
(the 720p copies on the site), measured at the pauses between paragraphs, so every cut falls in a pause.

## Order

1. Overview, paragraphs 1–18 (the bell, the koan, the three insights, why experience alone is not enough).
2. Steps 1 to 8, each starting with its spoken title card ("After Anatta 1. Two Logics." and so on) as a chapter marker.
   In every step the first sentence "This is the Nth step of The Logic, from After Anatta: notes by John Tan, known as
   Thusness." is cut (the title card has just said it); the rest of that paragraph stays.
3. The close: Step 8's last paragraph (the koan one last time), then the overview's last two paragraphs ("Listen to the
   bell once more…" and "Ask what arises, and the koan is a paradox…").

## Kept on purpose

2, 4, 5 and 7 (each turns it to its own question), the ways out in Steps 2, 5, 6 and 8, Mipham's cold of ice in Step 4,
"It is / it is not" in Step 6, and each step's own try-it and "beyond the moment" examples. Cutting those cleanly
would need new bridge sentences recorded; that could bring the video down to about 55 minutes, as a later round.
