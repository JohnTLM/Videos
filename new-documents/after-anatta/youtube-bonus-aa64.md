## Dependent Arising Section 6 Part 3: Elaboration Stops in Emptiness (AA64, 7:03)

Dependent Arising Section 6 Part 3: Elaboration Stops in Emptiness | Thusness

### Description

The chain ends when ignorance ends. But what was ignorance? Not a thing to be destroyed. A way of taking things.

The close of Section 6: the ending of the twelve links as Nagarjuna reads it. When this is not, that is not: the same pattern seen from its other side, so nothing real has to be broken. "This very knowledge" is seeing dependent arising as it is, which is seeing its emptiness: not only that what arises is not mine, but that arising itself is never found on its own. Action and affliction come from conceptual construction, and these from elaboration, the mind's spinning of "it is" and "it is not"; elaboration stops in emptiness, not in silence. Dependent arising as a view is the antidote to ignorance, not its shape. Samsara and nirvana are not two places. Emptiness is not a new thing to hold. And where the tradition and Nagarjuna agree, and where they part: for the tradition, the person is empty; for Nagarjuna, everything is.
