# Dependent Arising Section 2 Part 11: Where the Big Ideas Come From

A toothache. A sunrise. A key in a lock. Each one hides a big idea. Where do the big ideas come from?

I am John Tan, known as Thusness. These are my own notes, from my own practice and contemplation.

At the start of this section, I said that most of the big ideas we live by grow out of how we answer one question. What is it that appears? Now I want to show why.

Over ten parts, the answer has come into view. Whatever appears is vivid, not found, and dependently arisen, all at once. No floor is found under it, in time, in matter, or in space. None of its ways of appearing is the real one. Nor is the web of conditions. Its clarity needs no knower behind it. And everything depends completely, each event spending itself wholly as the meeting of its conditions.

The mind finds this hard to hold. Something fully present cannot be found. Something works, and when looked for, it is not there. So the mind fills the gap. What it fills it with, I believe, are the big ideas. Privacy. Objectivity. Causal power. Inside and outside.

These ideas are not mistakes made up from nothing. Each one starts from a genuine feature of how things appear. And then it misreads that feature as a thing. So I will not refute them by denying what they point to. I will look at what was added.

First, a toothache.

A tooth begins to ache. It throbs. It is unmistakable, and it is mine. You cannot feel it, however hard you try. I can tell you about it, and you can believe me. But the ache itself stays here.

Surely, then, there is a private room behind my eyes. An inner theatre, where my experiences happen. The ache is in there. My thoughts, my colours, my sounds are in there too. Each of us has one, sealed off from all the others.

Or, and it is a big or, look at what the privacy actually shows.

The ache depends on this tooth, these nerves, this attention. Your ache would depend on your tooth, your nerves, your attention. So there is a real asymmetry of access. What arises with this body arises with this body, and not with yours. That is valid, and it works. The dentist relies on it, when asking me where it hurts.

But look for the room. Where is the inner theatre the ache is supposed to be in? Not in the tooth. Not in the nerves. Not in the brain, as a box with a screen. The vividness of the ache is undeniable, and no owner is found holding it. The clarity belongs to the aching itself. Not to a viewer sitting behind it.

So privacy is a real feature of appearing. An asymmetry of conditions. A hidden room is not found.

And nothing is missing. The tooth still aches. I still go to the dentist. And you still cannot feel it for me.

Second, a sunrise.

Every morning, the sun comes up over the hill. It came up yesterday. It will come up tomorrow. A thousand people on the hill would all point to the same place in the sky.

Surely, then, the world is objective, and given. It is out there, already complete, waiting to be seen. Seeing only reports it. And the agreement of all those people proves it.

Or, and again it is a big or, look at what the regularity actually shows.

Appearances repeat. Similar conditions, meeting again and across many people, give similar appearances. The same sky. The same hill. The same eyes. The same kind of body. The same turning of the earth.

Section 1 took this apart at length. Regularity is the coherence of dependent arising, not evidence of a thing behind it. Agreement is what conventional truth is. Not proof of a floor. And nothing is given before the conditions, because the conditions include the knowing. The eye, the light, the attention, and the kind of being that sees.

So objectivity is a real feature of appearing. Things appear reliably, and in common. A world given in advance, before any seeing, is not found.

And nothing is missing. The sun still rises. The farmer still plants by it. And the thousand people still agree.

Third, a key.

I put a key in a lock, and turn it. The door opens. The key did it.

Surely, then, the key has a power inside it. The power to open. Causes are things with power, and the power passes from the cause into the effect.

Or, and it is the biggest or, look for the power.

Hold the key alone, away from the door. It opens nothing. Put it in the wrong lock. Nothing. Put it in the right lock, with no hand to turn it. Nothing. The power to open is found only with the lock, the hand, the turning and the door, all together. Take any one away, and the power is nowhere.

So causal efficacy is itself a relation. Things arise when conditions meet. And the habit reads this as one thing pushing another, with a force stored inside. But the mark once taken as proof of independent reality, it acts, so it must be real in itself, is the clearest mark of dependence. And even the conditions are not found standing by themselves.

And nothing is missing. The key still opens the door. Fire still burns. Medicine still heals. And actions still bear fruit.

Fourth, inside and outside.

Look around. Some things appear near, and some far. Some are felt from within. A pang of hunger. A mood. Some are touched from without. A table. A wall.

Surely there are two boxes. Inside is the mind, with its feelings and thoughts. Outside is matter, with its tables and walls. Each is complete on its own side. And the deep puzzle is how the two connect.

Or look again. Near and far, felt and touched, are ways of appearing. Searched for, neither box is found. The inner box is not found as a container of thoughts. The outer box is not found as a container of walls. Mind and matter are two cuts in one appearing. And neither stands on its own side more than the other.

This is only a sketch. The next section of the series, Mind and Matter, gives it the room it needs.

And nothing is missing. Hunger is still felt within. The wall still stops me, without. And I still eat.

So, which holds?

By experience. I meet the ache, the sunrise, the opening door, the near and the far. At no point do I meet a private room. A world given before seeing. A power stored in a key. Or two sealed boxes. What is met is always the appearing itself, vivid, and in its conditions.

By logic. A private room would have to be somewhere. And it is found neither in the body, nor apart from it. A given world would have to be like something, before any seeing. A power stored in a cause would act without its conditions. And it never does.

By working. The big ideas explain nothing that dependent arising does not explain better. Privacy is the asymmetry of conditions. Objectivity is their reliable coherence. Causal power is their meeting. Inside and outside are ways of appearing. Everything works. And nothing extra is needed to make it work.

So the big ideas, taken as things, are not the truer view. They are the default one, carried by habit and by language. The my, of my ache. The the, of the world. The power, of the key's power. And the in here, of in my mind.

None of this makes my everyday words false. The ache is mine. The world is shared. The key opens. The wall is out there. What fails is only the thing each word quietly sets up, behind the appearing.

What Section 2 has shown.

What appears is vivid, not found, and dependently arisen, all at once. What is it in itself, falls away. No floor is found. Not the moment, not the atom, not space. No way of appearing is privileged, and the web of conditions is not the floor either. The clarity is the appearing itself. Everything depends, completely, each event spending itself wholly as the meeting of its conditions. And the big ideas are features of appearing, misread as things.

And two traps sit on either side of all of this.

Too much. It is vivid, so it really exists. The self has gone, but the appearances are kept as little things standing on their own. Or pure presence quietly becomes the new ground. This is the trap that waits just after anatta.

Too little. It is not found, so it is nothing. The ache, the sunrise and the medicine are denied, and emptiness becomes a blank behind things. But the absence is not a thing. And the appearing was never denied. Only its standing on its own.

Recognition, not search.

The searches of this section do real work. They catch the habit in the act. Each time the mind looks for the floor and finds nothing, the sense that there must be one loosens a little.

But the search is not where it ends. What appears does not become unfindable when the search is finished. It was never findable. And searching can harden into a habit of its own. A dividing mind that keeps one last place for itself. The place from which it searches.

What I have come to value more is recognition. When appearing is recognised as it is, nothing needs to be looked for. Each appearance is met as vivid, not found, and releasing itself as it comes. Like a drawing on water. With nothing to hold, and no one holding.

Recognition is not an achievement. And the moment it is held as an accomplishment, the fixing has returned. Nor is it passive. It still takes clear knowing. In my experience, it is recognised again and again. Not reached once, and kept.

And then the ordinary world is simply the ordinary world. The tooth aches. The sun rises. The key turns. The bell rings. Nothing is lost. What has gone is only the floor, and the room, and the power, and the knower, that were never there.

The tradition has a word for meeting appearances this way. Wonder. Colours, sounds, tastes, thoughts, cars and birds appear, vivid and precise, whenever their conditions come together. And none of them is found standing anywhere. That is not a problem to be solved. It is magical. And miraculous.
