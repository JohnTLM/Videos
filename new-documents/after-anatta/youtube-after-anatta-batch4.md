## 3. What Is Negated (After Anatta 2, 8:42)

### Title

What Is Negated: Own-Nature, Not the World | After Anatta 2 · Your Own Weight

### Description

The answer to the koan is that no "it" is found. But what it denies is narrow. Tsongkhapa made this the first step of the whole analysis: unless the target is found as it appears, the negation misses.

The second step of The Logic: what is denied is own-nature, svabhāva, and nothing else. The sound is still heard. Two ways to fall short (refuting only theories, or keeping a pure Awareness just behind the self), the opposite mistake of nihilism, the non-implicative negation that puts nothing in its place, and why presence was never the target. Then your own weight: stand on a scale, and ask where the weight is. Once dependent arising is seen, mind and matter lose their weight and become light, magical and plastic.

The four corners are medicine, not forbidden words, and what remains when they fall is not a blank. Then an ambulance passes: is the siren high or low?
