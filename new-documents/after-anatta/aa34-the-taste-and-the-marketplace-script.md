# The Taste and the Marketplace

In Negation and Formation, freedom turned out to be in conventions, not a leap into silence. Words and names keep working. The painted rice cake feeds.

I am John Tan, known as Thusness. These are my own notes, from my own practice and contemplation.

That raises two questions. If conventions keep working, what exactly is the problem with them? And all of this is view. How is it actually met, in a life?

Start with the first.

There are two ways of knowing rightly here. It helps not to collapse them.

The first works through concepts, within the framework. It is how a doctor treats, how a cook cooks, how a promise is kept, how karma is understood. This is not a lesser knowing.

The second is freedom from all elaborations. Nothing reified is added. Nothing is cut into before and after, part and whole, cause and effect. One complete stroke. One does not mean one stuff. It means not cut up. The colour is still the colour, and the sound is still the sound.

Neither cancels the other. The first is not a ladder to throw away. The second is not a place outside the world to retreat to.

So I keep two frameworks apart.

The default framework is the conventional cutting itself: parts and whole, before and after, cause and effect, agent and action. It works. We need it to speak, to cook and to keep promises. Dependent arising lives here.

The inherent framework is the habit of taking what the cutting singles out as standing by itself. That is the matrix. Not a world, not a field. A habit of seeing.

The framework is not the prison. What binds is living in it with reified thoughts. Dependent arising lives in the first, and unbinds the second. Run the two together, and, dependent arising lives in a framework, is heard as, dependent arising is only a story. That is the slide into annihilation.

So the framework can be held three ways. Without it: one complete stroke. Within it, with the wisdom of dependent arising: the same parts and conditions, now light. And in between, where we mostly live: within it, reified.

Have a second thought. If you think you do not think inherently, have a second thought. Toss an apple. Is it simply there, before any thought at all? For almost all of us, it is. That is the habit at work. It does not feel like a view. It feels like the world itself. So agreeing with dependent arising is not living in it.

Which brings the second question. How is it met?

For me, dependent arising did not come first as a view. It came as a taste, and the view came to sync with it later. Looked back on, the taste was met three times. Right after anatta. Again when every hinge was exhausted. And again in the marketplace.

Right after anatta, there is only seeing, no seer. But then, what are seeing and hearing? Look, in real time.

Seeing is just colours. No effort at all. Hearing is just sound. No effort at all. Nothing is doing the seeing, and the seeing is not being done. Nothing runs underneath the colours, carrying them from one moment to the next.

And the colours are not dark. They are clear, known and vivid. The knowing is right there in the colour, and nothing stands behind it, knowing.

How is this different from a refined non-dual state? A refined non-dual rests in oneness. Everything is made of one luminosity. The seer has merged, but the one it merged into is still a something. Anatta finds no seer there to merge. So in this taste there is no made of. The colour is clear as colour, with light and eye. The sound is clear as sound, with air and ear. Six clarities, one for each stream. Not one lamp lighting them all.

The taste widens. Each event seems to spend itself completely. The sound of a car going by is wholly the sound, with nothing behind it holding back. Dōgen called this total exertion: firewood in its own place, ash in its own. Not the whole cosmos packed into each moment. Just this, spent completely.

But a taste is not yet freedom.

While I see a flower, the seeing is just colours, with no one behind it. Yet the moment I turn away, the flower is still assumed to be sitting there by itself, its red belonging to it. A cause is still felt to push its effect. Death is still the end of a someone. The self had dissolved, but the universe had not.

For years after anatta, the taste was there, and the view still treated each thing as standing by itself. That gap is what I call the desynchronisation.

The habit does not announce itself. It is only when it is challenged by Nāgārjuna's verses that it comes to the surface. The logic points to the place to look. Then I look, and check it against the taste. Insight without the logic drifts back into nouns. Logic without the taste stays a clever argument about words.

Before freedom, the mind will always hinge on something. First it hinges on parts. When the parts are seen through, it hinges on the whole. When the whole is seen through, it finds something finer to stand on. Qi. Energy. A field. Relations. A substrate. Each time, it is looking for a foundation.

The tools meet each hinge in turn. When all of these are exhausted, neither parts nor whole, with nothing left to hinge on, the mind becomes free from all elaborations, and in a stable way. Stable does not mean the habit can never stir again. When it stirs, it is met by the same seeing, and it does not take root.

Then appearance is again tasted as vividly present, spontaneous and free. It is the first taste, revisited. What has changed is its reach, now covering thought, the body, causes, time and death. And its stability: it no longer depends on looking.

After freedom from all elaborations, I re-enter the marketplace, and dirty my hands.

The marketplace is ordinary life. Work, family, money, arguments, other people's troubles, choices. The last of the Zen ox-herding pictures shows it: barefoot, smeared with mud and ashes, he comes into the market with helping hands, smiling broadly.

Here dependent arising changes its role. Before, it was the tool that took each hinge apart. Now I deal in causes and conditions all day. What helps, what harms, what follows from what. Dependent arising is how help works.

Who decides, if no one is there? Nobody disappears. I still act, choose, keep my promises and answer for what I do, as a conventional person. What is gone is the doer behind the doing, pulling the levers. No one is there, so nothing matters, is not freedom. It is the annihilation view.

And why help, if others are not found? Others suffer, validly, in dependence. Their suffering is eased when its conditions change. And looked for, no one is found suffering from their own side, so nothing in it is fixed for good.

So, looked back on, the taste has been met three times. These are not stages. The logic does not produce the taste. It unbinds the habit. And it is not a ladder climbed once. It goes round again, wherever the habit of fixing returns.

And the taste has three descriptions. Anatta: no one is behind it. Dependent arising: nothing in it stands on its own. Spontaneous presence: nothing is missing, and nothing is making it. Three angles on one face. None is higher.

No one behind it. Nothing standing alone. Nothing missing. Met in the colours, in the hinges, and in the marketplace, with dirty hands.
