### Description

A painful feeling is the first arrow. What we do with it is the second.

The close of Section 5: how the twelve links stop, in the tradition's own terms. The reverse order: remove a condition, and what stood on it does not arise; the chain need not be broken link by link. Two places to watch: ignorance at the start, ended by knowing the four truths, and the step from feeling to craving in the middle. The second arrow: the wise still feel pain; what ends is the craving and the story added to it, so the cut is not numbness. Ignorance ends by seeing, not by stopping thought: the problem is the habit of fixing. What ends is becoming, not the world; a fire that goes out has not gone east or west. A chain that runs the other way, from suffering to faith, gladness, calm and freedom. And the harsh word of Part 1, watched instead of obeyed.
