## Undeniably There, or Formed? (AA35, 9:56)

### Title

Dependent Arising, Part 1: Undeniably There, or Formed? | Thusness

### Description

"If you think you understand quantum mechanics, you don't understand quantum mechanics." The same holds for dependent arising. I can agree that nothing has an essence of its own and still hold the world exactly as before: solid, out there, mine. Agreeing is not living in it.

So I set two ways of seeing side by side, three times. An apple: tossed, held, bitten, undeniably objective. Or six dependently originated streams of consciousness meeting, and named "apple" in the meeting: not what it really is, but how it forms. A mountain: massive, there before me. Or formed: a blue shape from the valley, aching legs on the path, contour lines on a map, and a name that draws an edge where nature drew none. A bat, a bee and an ant: one mountain seen many ways, or a different body singling out a different world.

Then the test: which carries more valid truth, by experience, by logic, and by how things work? The first view is the default, carried by habit, not by evidence. Everyday claims stay true; only "from its own side" fails. Nothing that works is lost. Only a floor that was never there is gone.

Part 1 of a series on dependent arising. Next, Part 2: The Illusion of the Given.
