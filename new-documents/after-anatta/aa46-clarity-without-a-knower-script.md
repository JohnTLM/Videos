# Dependent Arising Section 2 Part 5: Clarity Without a Knower

Everything else has been looked for, and not found. But surely not the one who finds.

I am John Tan, known as Thusness. These are my own notes, from my own practice and contemplation.

In Parts 1 to 4, what appears was looked for, and not found. Not a bell. Not a red. Not a thought. No floor in time, in matter, or in space. No privileged way of appearing. And not the web of conditions either.

One candidate is left. And the habit saves it for last, because it seems to be the one doing all the looking.

Every appearance is clear. The colour is seen as colour. The sound is heard as sound. The thought is known as thought. This clarity is not one more kind of thing among the others. It runs through all appearing. The solid, the dreamlike, and the described alike.

Surely, then, there is a light behind it all. A knower. A witness. A background awareness that receives the appearances and lights them up. The one constant behind the changing show. Everything else may be unfindable. But surely not the one who finds.

Or look for it.

Look at a tree. There is the tree, and there is seeing. Now look for a viewer, standing apart from both. It is not found.

Listen to the bell. There is the ringing, clear and complete. Look for a listener behind it. The ringing does not need one to be clear. The clarity is the appearing's own. It is not borrowed from a knower behind it.

This is the observer returning, in a quieter form, after anatta has taken it apart once. And it is the easiest place for a new floor to grow. Pure awareness. The witness. The ground of being. Each sounds like a deep realisation. Each makes the clarity into a thing, standing behind the appearing.

But this is not only an argument. It is about something tasted. So I want to say, as plainly as I can, what experience is like when there is no layer.

When no layer is left.

If there is no substrate, no layer underneath, what happens to experience? The habit expects it to grow thin. Or to vanish. It is the other way round. Nothing is lost, except the distance.

As long as a layer is assumed, experience is always one step removed. There is me, in here. A screen, or a mind, in between. And the world, out there. The sound reaches me through the ear, is turned into a picture of sound, and is then known by someone. Every experience comes with a middleman.

When the layer is seen through, the middleman goes. Nothing stands between the hearing and the sound, because there is no between left for anything to stand in.

The Buddha put it to Bāhiya as a direct taste. In the seen, just the seen. In the heard, just the heard. Not a seer who sees the seen. Just the seen. Vivid, and complete.

So yes. It has to be direct, and without anything in between. And the clarity is exactly the appearances. Not a clarity that has sounds, or contains colours, or shines on thoughts. The ringing is the clarity. The red is the clarity. The thought is the clarity.

There is no knowing left over, once the sound is fully heard. And no sound left over that the knowing has not already been. Knowing and known are not two. Not because they have melted into one thing. But because they were never found as two things in the first place.

And the clarity is not one even light, poured over everything. Each appearance is clear in its own way. The clarity of the bell is the ringing. The clarity of the red is redness. The clarity of an ache is aching. Sound never becomes colour. Thought never becomes warmth. Intimate without merging. Distinct without walls.

What is this like, in taste? I can only point.

Vivid. Without a layer to soften it, each appearance is fully itself. The bell rings with a brightness that was always there, but used to be held at a distance.

Intimate. There is no gap between here and there to cross. The tree is not over there, being seen from here. The scenery is the seeing.

Without centre, and without edge. No point of view sits at the middle of experience, looking out. And experience has no border, where it stops and the world begins.

Effortless. Nobody is doing the hearing. Hearing happens when the conditions meet. And it is complete, without help.

Self releasing. Each appearance is complete, and gone. Like a drawing on water, which needs no eraser. Nothing is kept, so nothing needs to be let go.

And one caution. Because this is exactly where it goes wrong.

A non dual experience can still be held in a substantialist way. The split between knower and known collapses. And then the openness that is left, the pure presence, the luminous awareness, quietly becomes the new floor. Luminosity takes the seat that substance used to occupy.

But the clarity is empty too. Looked for, it is not found. And it is just as vivid for that. A taste that has no ground under it does not need one. And the moment it is held as a possession, a layer has come back.

The Dzogchen tradition has its own words for this clarity. And I find them precious, when they are read without a ground.

Luminosity is not a bright substance behind the display. It is the knowing clarity of the display itself. Display is not the display of something hidden. It is appearing, vivid and unobstructed, with nothing behind it. Spontaneous presence is the completeness of each appearance, owing nothing to a hidden source. And self release is the drawing on water.

I should say plainly that this is a reading. Some in the Nyingma tradition speak of the clear nature of mind as the basis itself, and set ordinary mind apart from rigpa, as wisdom. They will find more in those words than I take from them here. I take them in the sense that rules out a ground, because that is how I have come to see it.

And nothing is missing. The tree is still seen. The bell is still heard. The thought is still known. Only the one standing behind them is not found. And was never needed.

So, which holds?

By experience. Look at a tree. Listen to the bell. Notice a thought. There is the tree and seeing. The ringing and hearing. The thought and knowing. At no point is a viewer met, standing apart from them, receiving them from behind. What is met is the clarity of each appearance, in its own way.

By logic. A knower standing behind experience would have to be picked out. Picked out, it becomes one more appearance, known by something further back. And the regress never ends. Or it is never picked out. And then nothing at all has been found to stand there. Either way, no witness is established.

By working. Seeing sees. Hearing hears. Thinking thinks. And all of it works without a viewer in the middle. The witness explains nothing that the clarity of appearing does not already do.

So the knower behind the knowing is not the truer view. It is the default one, carried by habit, and by one little word. The I in I see.

None of this makes my everyday words false. I see the tree. I hear the bell. What fails is only the one standing behind the seeing, holding it.
