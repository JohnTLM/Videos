## 1. Mind and Consciousness: Both Are Also Singled Out (AA20, 17:42)

### Title

Mind and Consciousness: Both Are Also Singled Out | After Anatta

### Description

Why I do not say that mind is the empty vivid appearing. Whatever appears is singled out as "this", and the singling out is the appearing itself, already dependence, already empty. Mind is singled out too, and so are consciousness and luminous clarity. Their characteristics follow the act that singles them out and the framework they fit: everyday talk, the Abhidharma, Yogācāra, neuroscience. Matter is singled out the same way, so the Hard Problem's gap was made by its starting point.

Clarity is never absent from what appears, but it is not the floor: lifted out and held, it becomes the last refuge of the self. The phase "appearances are one's radiance clarity", between phases 4 and 5, was a direct taste read inside the old framework. Bare attention gives the taste, not the view; anatta completes that course. After anatta the path goes from the desynchronisation, through refining and syncing the view, to spontaneous perfection. Then the habit and the matrix; how this stands with Nāgārjuna, Dzogchen and Mahāmudrā (ordinary mind and rigpa); and why spontaneous presence is dependent arising, like Dōgen's firewood and ash.
