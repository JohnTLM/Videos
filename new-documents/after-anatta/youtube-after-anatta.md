## 1. What the Logic Is Looking At (AA12, 7:12)

### Title

What the Logic Is Looking At: Dharma, Designation and the World of Only Blue | After Anatta

### Description

Listen to a sound nearby: a car, a bird, the hum of a fridge. It is vivid and clear, and it needs no help to be known. Every step of the logic looks at something: a self, an object, a cause, matter, a person. They are not many topics. Each time, the logic is looking at a phenomenon, what Buddhism calls a dharma.

This video says what a phenomenon is: whatever can be experienced or known, conditioned or unconditioned. It shows why there is no bare appearance under the names, why designation needs no words, only singling out, and why singling out is already dependence. Picture a world of only blue: no one there would ever see blue. So by appearing, a thing is already empty. Then the clarity: not a light behind the scene, and not a Self, but the appearing's own, as empty as everything else.
