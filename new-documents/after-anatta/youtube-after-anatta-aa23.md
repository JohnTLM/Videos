## 1. Part 1 of 6: If You Think You Understand (AA23, 14:30)

### Title

Dependent Arising, Part 1 of 6: If You Think You Understand | After Anatta

### Description

"If you think you understand quantum mechanics, you don't understand quantum mechanics." The same trap waits with dependent arising. On paper it sounds straightforward: when this is, that is. It reads like ordinary cause and effect, and fits easily onto the everyday, objective picture of the world. But is it really that straightforward?

Two ordinary examples, looked at closely. An apple tossed and caught: looked for, the solid, independent apple is not found, only many appearings met together, designated on a basis, and the toss still works perfectly. A mountain: does a bat, a bee or an ant see the same mountain? Body and world arise together, and measuring is no view from nowhere. Then the two words as the tradition defines them, dependent arising and designation, and why it is stranger than it sounds: free of the eight extremes, vivid yet nowhere found, long only beside short, and no power found sitting in a cause.
