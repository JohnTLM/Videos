## Dependent Arising Section 1 Part 3: Measured, or Formed? (AA37, 10:53)

### Title

Dependent Arising Section 1 Part 3: Measured, or Formed? | Thusness

### Description

A detector clicks: up. Was the electron up all along? Physics, surely, measures the world as it is, from no one's side. I am not a physicist; I set two views beside dependent arising as fairly as I can, Carlo Rovelli's relational quantum mechanics and Karen Barad's agential realism, as echoes, not proofs.

A measured value: read off like the time from a clock, or a fact that holds relative to the system that met it. Two observers compare notes: agreement as proof of the given, or agreement formed in the meeting between you, me and the electron, as Rovelli and Emily Adlam put it. A stick in a dark room: a fixed line between observer and observed, or Barad's agential cut, which moves with how the stick is held.

Then where I part from them. Read as "things are not real, but relations are", the echo only moves the floor. A relation needs what it relates; relations and the things related arise together, and neither comes first. Vivid, working, and empty all the way through, the relations included. Then the test, by experience, logic and working: the given is not the truer view, even in physics.
