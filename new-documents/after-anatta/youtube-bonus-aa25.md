## What It Is For (AA25, 14:56)

### Title

Dependent Arising, Further Viewing: What It Is For | Thusness

### Description

What is dependent arising for? I keep two ways of knowing rightly apart: through concepts, within the framework, and free from all elaborations, one complete stroke. Neither cancels the other. Within the framework, dependent arising explains how things come about, dismantles what seems to stand from its own side, and describes the taste: nothing standing on its own, nothing missing. It puts nothing in place of what it removes, and it dismantles itself too. Agreeing with it is not living in it. Toss the apple: is it simply there, before any thought at all?

Ignorance is not a power either. It is a deep habit of confused seeing, and it can end because nothing stands behind it. Dependent arising is the antidote to ignorance, not its shape, which is why the masters praise it. The word for conventional, samvrti, has three meanings: concealment, mutual dependence and worldly convention. What veils is ignorance, never dependent arising. I state other readings fairly, and I give one thread, the five lights, as my own contemplation, with its own disclaimer.
