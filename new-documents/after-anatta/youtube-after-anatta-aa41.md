## Rainbow Body: What the Traditions Report (AA41, 11:37)

### Title

Rainbow Body: What the Traditions Report | After Anatta

### Description

A master dies in a small room in eastern Tibet, and over a week, the story goes, the body grows smaller until only hair and nails are left. This video only reports: what the traditions say the rainbow body is, how they say it is reached, what the reports of it are, and where other traditions tell something like it.

Dzogchen: the basis in three aspects, the five lights hardening into the elements, cutting through and direct crossing, the four visions and the lamps, the three results, and the teachers' own warning that the rainbow body is first a realization. Gelug: mind and wind, the stages of dying, the illusory body, union, and tukdam with the 2021 study. Kālacakra's empty form and the divining mirror. Bön's own rainbow body, and tables that differ from text to text. The modern reports and their lists. The Taoist yang spirit, Lü Dongbin and Huanglong, others who tell of a body of light, and the early discourses on powers. A Tibetan image: the leather butter bag. And one ordinary moment, holding an apple.
