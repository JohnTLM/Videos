## The Taste and the Marketplace (AA34, 8:55)

### Title

Dependent Arising, Part 4 of 4: The Taste and the Marketplace | Thusness

### Description

If conventions keep working, what exactly is the problem with them? I keep two ways of knowing apart: through concepts, within the framework, and free from all elaborations, one complete stroke. And two frameworks: the conventional cutting itself, which works and where dependent arising lives, and the habit of taking what the cutting singles out as standing by itself. The framework is not the prison. What binds is living in it with reified thoughts. Toss an apple: is it simply there, before any thought at all? Agreeing with dependent arising is not living in it.

For me, dependent arising came first as a taste, and the view came to sync with it later. The taste was met three times. Right after anatta: seeing is just colours, with no one behind it and no "made of". Again when every hinge was exhausted, after years when the taste was there and the view still treated each thing as standing by itself. And again in the marketplace, ordinary life, where dependent arising is how help works. These are not stages. No one behind it, nothing standing alone, nothing missing.

Part 4 of 4. Previous: Negation and Formation. Start from Part 1: The Fist, the Wind and the Chariot.
