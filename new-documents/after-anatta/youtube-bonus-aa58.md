### Title

Dependent Arising Section 4 Part 3: Continuity Without a Carrier | Thusness

### Description

If nothing is carried from moment to moment, what makes a life one life? A habit one habit?

The close of Section 4, The Logic of Dependency. Two pictures are both wrong: a carrier that persists, and disconnected flashes. Nagasena's lamp through the watches of the night is neither the same flame nor another; a melody known by heart is recognised across every playing with no tune stored between. Nothing is lost: memory, responsibility and karma hold. Nothing is fixed: a habit can loosen and a grievance can end. Energy is not the carrier: it is a conserved quantity, frame-dependent, from a symmetry. Potentiality is not the carrier either: "could" is said within conditions, and plasticity has limits. The path under each logic: a traveller removing and acquiring states, or arranging conditions, with openness itself held lightly. And back to the candle: exactly as much light as before.
