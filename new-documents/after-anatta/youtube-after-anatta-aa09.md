## 1. Decoding Anatta and MMK Chapter 3 (AA09, about 21:09)

### Title

Decoding Anatta and MMK Chapter 3: Why Dependent Arising Is Spontaneous Presence | After Anatta

### Description

The Buddha told Bāhiya: in the seen, just the seen. Nāgārjuna says: without a seer, there is no seen, and without the seen, no seer. One seems to take the seer away, the other to put it back. There is no contradiction. One is the direct insight; the other is how the logic of dependency expresses the same insight and taste.

John Tan (Thusness) shows what falls away in anatta: not the person, but a doer behind the doing. Nāgārjuna makes the same move in MMK chapters 2, 3, 10 and 1: the walker, the seer, fire and fuel, cause and effect, each found as neither the same as its action nor other than it. Found as neither is what "empty" means, and the car still drives you home. His no puts nothing in its place, not a big Self, not pure awareness, not a web of conditions. With no doer anywhere, appearing is already complete: dependent arising is spontaneous presence. And clarity is never missing; the only question is whether it is lifted out and made into a thing.
