### Description

A harsh word is said in the morning. By evening, a grievance has a life of its own. How did that happen?

Section 5 turns to the oldest meaning of dependent arising, as the tradition teaches it: how suffering comes about, and how it ends. First the one sentence beneath it all: when this is, that is; when this is not, that is not. It says only "when": nothing is passed across. Then the twelve links, from ignorance to ageing and death, in forward order and in reverse. The chain is not a line of dominoes with a first one standing on its own, but two sheaves of reeds leaning on each other. Three ways of reading it: across three lives, in four ways, and in the moment. And one afternoon, a harsh word traced link by link into a grievance. Deep, and appearing deep.
