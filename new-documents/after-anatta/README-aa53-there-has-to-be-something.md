## Scenes

1. Rain at a window; "What is it, really?"; the title; before going further.
2. Three answers: matter, mind, a third stuff (energy, field, qi).
3. There has to be something: taken as reality; materialist and idealist agree there must be one.
4. The grammar of things: the rose is red, the lightning flashes, "it" is raining, with a dashed box and a question mark. The "it" is never struck or answered (owner: "don't break the it").
5. The three questions; analyse, explain, orient; words used, not believed.
6. Recognise it first: tinted glasses.
7. The Middle Way's completely new approach, in five points; endless negation struck.
8. Against experience: the stuff it is made of, struck; never met.
9. Against itself: floors going down; the bearer; the ground struck.
10. Only a habit; two cautions.
11. The rain was only ever raining.
