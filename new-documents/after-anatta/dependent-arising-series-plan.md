## Three rules for every part

1. **One home per idea and per example.** Explained once; elsewhere recalled in one sentence at most.
2. **No gap.** Every term explained where it first appears; each part ends on the question the next part answers.
3. **The corrected wording throughout.** No "despite", "yet it still", "not found, yet it carries"; never "because
   empty, it works".

## The seven parts

Title card "Dependent Arising · Part N of 7". About 12 to 19 minutes each.

### Part 1. If You Think You Understand (from AA23)

- The apple tossed from hand to hand; the mountain, the bat and the surveyor; "when no one looks".
- The two words defined: dependence and designation, one fact from two sides; long and short; subject and object.
- Leaves to Part 4: the "undeniable" habit and physics (AA31 §1).
- **Ends on:** it is not found, and it works. How? First, what dependent arising says.

### Part 2. What It Says: Conditions, With No Doer (from AA24, AA21)

- Five mishearings: a web, a machine, only concepts, a lower truth, the shape of ignorance.
- These conditions, met together, and this appears: the bell, the match. Causal efficacy without causal power; three
  senses of cause.
- Continuity with no carrier (the lamp, MMK 18.10); meeting with no glue (hand and ice, MMK 14.8).
- Neither mind nor matter. Anatta: no doer behind seeing; Nāgārjuna makes the same move everywhere.
- The search for a bottom layer: the Abhidharma's dharmas, "bearing their own mark".
- Leaves to Part 3: designation, the chariot, MMK 15 (an essence could not arise).
- **Ends on:** if no doer stands behind the conditions, what is the thing we name, and why does "unfindable, yet it
  works" sound so wrong?

### Part 3. The Fist, the Wind and the Chariot (AA32, whole)

- As built: the "despite" trap; anatta sees one side; the four insights; the breeze; the disservice; how I say it
  now; Nietzsche; Nāgasena's chariot; the open hand.
- Changes: title card; first line recalls Part 2's question; the ending line points on ("and still one question
  hides in every question").

### Part 4. Negation and Formation (AA31, whole)

- As built: two halves; the "undeniable"; three strands; the five steps (no "what", the eight extremes, the "it" slips
  in, the "it" is formed, drop the "it"); three features of every appearance; the apple at dusk; mind and matter; the
  "it" never needed; no leap into silence; DN 9, SN 1.25, Tsongkhapa, Dōgen.
- Changes: title card; its brief cause, continuity and meeting passages shortened to one recall of Part 2; "Next:"
  line.
- **Ends on:** so what is it for?

### Part 5. What It Is For (from AA25, AA21)

- Two ways of knowing rightly; the framework held three ways; the default framework and the inherent habit.
- It explains, it dismantles, it describes the taste.
- Ignorance: dependent arising as its antidote; Candrakīrti on the "veil".
- Freedom from elaborations recalled with Part 4's "no leap": one sentence, not repeated.
- **Ends on:** all this is view. How is it met?

### Part 6. The Taste (from AA26, AA22)

- Met three times: after anatta (seeing is just colours; no "made of"; total exertion); when every hinge is
  exhausted; in the marketplace.
- Part 4's three strands recalled in one sentence; the hinges in full here.
- **Ends on:** one taste; how many faces?

### Part 7. One Taste, Many Faces (from AA27, AA22)

- Three descriptions, one taste; six tastes ("vividly appearing and unfindable"…); many faces; the guards; the
  masters and where I part; try it.
- **Ends:** recalling the open hand of Part 3.
