## Scenes

1. Everything else not found: surely not the one who finds? The title.
2. A knower behind the clarity? Look for it: a tree and seeing (the dashed viewer fades), the bell and its ringing; new floors named and struck.
3. When no layer is left: me | screen | world, the middleman goes; in the seen, just the seen; the clarity is exactly the appearances; each clear in its own way.
4. The taste: vivid, intimate, without centre or edge, effortless, self-releasing (a drawing on water).
5. One caution: non-duality held as a new floor; the clarity is empty too.
6. The Dzogchen words read without a ground; the Nyingma note.
7. Which holds?
