## The Mind Trap and the Bottom Layer (AA33, 8:33)

### Title

Dependent Arising, Part 2 of 4: The Mind Trap and the Bottom Layer | Thusness

### Description

Even after the chariot is seen to be only designated, the habit looks for a floor somewhere else. In my experience it hides in three places: under the parts, inside the mind, and in the world when no one is looking. The Abhidharma set out to find the bottom layer, dharmas that each bear their own mark. Nāgārjuna shows that mark and marked stand or fall together, and that a dharma with its own nature could not arise, change or cease. Even the hardness of an apple is singled out on touch, skin and pressure. And the apple is hard, and the hand knows it.

Strike a bell. The ting is the hearing, and that is an important insight. The trap is what tends to come next: folding everything into consciousness, a universal Awareness. Matter traded for mind is still stuff. Then a mountain, as a bat, a bee and an ant meet it. The mountain is still there when I look away and look back. That it exists from its own side, whether or not anything meets it, was never shown. Reliability is not independence.
