### Description

Watch a candle burn, and the answer comes before any thought: the flame is changing. Something is there, and something is happening to it. That answer is a whole logic.

The opening of Section 4, The Logic of Dependency. The logic of substance starts with things and asks what happens to them; the logic of dependency asks how anything is identified as a thing at all. "Logic" here means the necessity of a consequence: not a survey, a negation without a thesis, and keeping the ordinary rules of non-contradiction. The famous refusals, neither existent nor non-existent, refuse a presupposition, like asking whether the present King of France is bald. And change is met without a changer: the walker and the walking named together, no new passenger in the old seat, and the candle still burns down.
