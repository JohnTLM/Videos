## Dependent Arising Part 2: The Illusion of the Given (AA36, 12:12)

### Title

Dependent Arising Section 1 Part 2: The Illusion of the Given | Thusness

### Description

One bowl of liquid. A human sees water, a hungry ghost burning pus, a fish its home, a god nectar. Which one is really in the bowl? Even after the bat, the bee and the ant of Part 1, one belief survives: one finished world is there underneath, and each being only sees it through its own glasses. That belief is the given. It is not a theory I hold; it is how things seem before any thought.

So again I set two ways of seeing side by side, three times. A bowl: neutral stuff under four pairs of glasses, or the thing seen, the one who sees and the channel of meeting, designated together. A tick at a pool: the pool never forms in the tick's world, yet the water carries it when it falls in. Contact runs along channels, and comes in degrees. The box of space: an empty container, or a field designated together with what is in it.

"But we agree." Agreement is not proof of a hidden floor; it is what conventional truth is. Healthy senses correct each other within a kind, a circle with no judge outside it. The bowl does not prove the world is inside the mind; neither the world nor the mind is the floor. Then the test, by experience, logic and working. And if the ghost's pus is truly pus for the ghost, no being's lived world can be brushed aside.
