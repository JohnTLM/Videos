## 1. Causal Efficacy Without Causal Power (AA19, about 14 minutes)

### Title

Causal Efficacy Without Causal Power: Cause and Effect Seen Through | After Anatta

### Description

Strike a match, and there is a flame. Leave out the yeast, and the loaf is flat. Causes work. But a power inside them that makes the effect is never found. Both stand.

The habit of picturing a power in the cause is the same habit that builds a self: a doer behind the doing. This video takes it apart: the key that opens only its own lock, the three senses of "cause", and why a thing with a nature of its own could not act at all. Then the central point: conditions are the appearing. The parts appear as parts only in relation to a framework that cuts appearing up for explaining and for getting things done, and that framework works within agreements, inside a setting.

Is this orthodox? Checked against Nāgārjuna's Middle Way (chapters 1 and 20), Candrakīrti and Tsongkhapa's "able to perform a function", and why it is not Hume. Then karma across time: the mere I kept as the person the world speaks of, but not as a bridge; "neither carried nor cut", and the uneasy feeling of a missing gap, looked at until it is seen through. Last, Dzogchen's "beyond cause and effect", where I part from some readings, and how my view stands with Mipham.
