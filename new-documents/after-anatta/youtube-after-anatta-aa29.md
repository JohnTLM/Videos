## Rainbow Body: Four Papers, Read with Emptiness (AA29, 12:19)

### Title

Rainbow Body: Four Papers, Read with Emptiness | After Anatta

### Description

A body is left in a small room in eastern Tibet, and over a week it grows smaller until only hair and nails remain. Four papers by outside writers tell this story, from Bön, Dzogchen and Kālacakra. They tell it well. They also tell it in a way that can quietly bring back the very thing it is meant to free us from.

What the papers report well: the rainbow body and the great transference, cutting through and direct crossing, the four visions and the lamps. Then three slips. Light becomes a stuff: "the building block of the cosmos", matter as frozen light, awareness as a container. Read with the ground's empty essence, the five lights are display with nothing behind them, and nothing was ever frozen. Reports become facts: the case lists, tukdam and the 2021 study, and why the rainbow body is first a realization. Three traditions become one: Kālacakra's empty form, like an image in a divining mirror, union rather than a body of light, and Bön in its own voice. And a caution on health claims, and the Tibetan image of the leather butter bag.
