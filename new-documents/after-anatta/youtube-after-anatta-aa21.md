## 1. Dependent Arising and Designation (AA21, 17:01)

### Title

Dependent Arising and Designation: What It Is and What It Is For | After Anatta

### Description

Dependent arising runs through everything I write, and it is easy to mishear: as a web behind things, a machine of causes, "only concepts", a lower truth, or the shape of ignorance. This video sets out the view in one place and says what it is for. These conditions, met together, designated, working: things function fully, and no power is ever found sitting in a cause. It is the same seeing as anatta, turned outwards: no doer behind anything.

Designation is singling out. The chariot is not found among its parts or apart from them, and it still carries you; the parts are designated too, so no bottom layer is ever reached. The conditions are the appearing, and the relations are no more found than the things, so it is not a web. Two modes of right knowing; three uses (it explains, it dismantles and shows emptiness, it describes the taste); an enlightened view we do not yet live in. It does not originate with ignorance: it is the antidote to ignorance, which is why the masters praise it, and why what veils is ignorance, not dependent arising. And dependent arising is spontaneous presence: one appearing, described two ways, neither higher.
