## Negation and Formation (AA31, 18:57)

### Title

Dependent Arising, Part 3 of 4: Negation and Formation | Thusness

### Description

In the last video, the habit's hiding places were found empty. Now the wider map. Looking back over my recent articles, I see one shape running through all of them: two halves. Negation is seeing that nothing is found from its own side. Formation is understanding how a world without essence still appears, vivid, stable and working. Leaving the default habit of substance takes three strands, again and again: confirming it in direct experience, equipping the view with tools like Nagasena's chariot, and tasting the gradual loosening of grasping.

Then the trap. Having negated, the mind asks: then what are they? That question is the old view, still running, and it has to be seen through, not answered. With no substrate, "what is it" is invalid, the eight extremes do not apply, and the "it" is what is formed, not what forms. Drop the it, not the cup. Then the three features of every appearance, why empty does not mean weak, and the same two halves in cause, continuity, words and meeting. The "it" is never needed. And I do not take the leap into silence: with the Buddha's milk and curds, Tsongkhapa and Dogen's painted rice cake, words are used freely, and they work.
