## 1. The Logic of Dependency (AA17, about 14:33)

### Title

The Logic of Dependency: Change Without a Changer | After Anatta

### Description

In seeing, there is just the seen: no one stands behind the eyes. The logic of dependency is that same seeing turned on everything. Watch a candle burn, and the answer comes before any thought: the flame is changing, something is there and something is happening to it. Why does that picture come back, even after the arguments against it have been accepted?

Two logics use the same words and begin in different places. The logic of substance starts with things and asks what happens to them. The logic of dependency asks how anything is picked out as a thing at all. Do we really think in substances? Space as an empty room, time as a river, the passenger carried along, grammar, infants, emotion and grief. What "logic" means here: the force of a consequence, not a leap beyond logic. Then change without a changer, the eight extremes as eight ways of asking "where?", identity by criteria, convention as the only validity there is, and continuity without a carrier. Two objections: isn't energy the substrate, and isn't there a store of potentials? The path as arranging conditions, with nothing to hold. And where the logic ends: dependent arising is spontaneous presence.
