## Dependent Arising Section 2 Part 3: Space (AA43, 10:39)

### Title

Dependent Arising Section 2 Part 3: Space | Thusness

### Description

The moment and the atom are gone. Is space the last floor: one still, empty room that everything sits in?

Śāntarakṣita's test (Ornament of the Middle Way): whatever is really there must be really one or really many. Is the space around the bell the same as the space around the gate? Then the bell would be at the gate. Different? Then space has parts. Many pieces of space? Each piece has two sides again. Neither one nor many, like a face in a mirror: clearly there, not found. And "one courtyard", "three rooms" still work, because walls and doors mark them off.

Nāgārjuna comes from another side (MMK 5): could space be there before its mark, the absence of obstruction? Space and its mark are designated together, and space is neither existent nor non-existent. Two cautions: empty space is not emptiness, and the sky is an image, not a ground. Then three searches, one result: time, matter and space, and no floor in any of them.
