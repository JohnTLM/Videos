### Description

A passing sound is clear as it comes; nothing had to switch on. An old story tells of a body that shrinks to hair and nails. What could the one have to do with the other? Here I bring together what I have written, across my books and articles, on non-dual luminous appearances and the rainbow body. To read the rainbow body in a balanced way, neither dismissing nor inflating it, I looked into four things: the energy practices of different traditions, the five elements as Tibet and China each count them, the reports themselves, and what modern research finds about how the body knows.

Luminosity, for me, is the knowing clarity of appearing itself: not a light behind things, not a knower apart, and as empty as everything else. It is not brightness, and it is never missing; there are six clarities, not one lamp, so I say luminous appearances, not one luminous clarity. The error is lifting the sameness out and making it a witness, a mirror or a ground. I AM names luminous clarity itself; the sense of "I" added to it is the error, not the authentic clarity. Anatta freed what it relinquished, the self, but not yet the world; each deconstruction frees only what it has let go of, and even now the taste is still in oscillation. Vivid and not found are one fact, seen twice; spontaneous presence is dependent arising with no producer.

Then the body. The basis is empty of being a ground; the five lights are not a stuff, but the clarity of appearing in five qualities, to be read against the five elements of Indian and Tibetan thought, not modern ideas of light. Hold an apple: a habit ties hardness, red and sweetness into one solid thing. Untie the knot, and nothing is lost: what we call things are only the six lamps, unrecognised, the body included. The rainbow body is that knot untied all the way through, the body included: in essence an actualized realization, carried through waking, dreaming and deep sleep. Where I part: what frees is seeing through the inherent framework, not where the winds go. The body is not left out: the knot is held in the chest, the breath and the belly, and felt knowing is more immediate, not more fundamental. My balanced reading: what is shown is that the body takes part in knowing and in release; what is not shown is that a corpse turns into light. I hold the first with confidence, the second as a report. A body of light is not a goal.
