## Nothing Turns Into Light: Luminous Appearances and the Rainbow Body (AA40, 14:35)

### Title

Nothing Turns Into Light: Luminous Appearances and the Rainbow Body | After Anatta

### Description

A passing sound is clear as it comes; nothing had to switch on. An old story tells of a body that shrinks to hair and nails. What could the one have to do with the other? Here I bring together what I have written, across my books and articles, on non-dual luminous appearances and the rainbow body.

Luminosity, for me, is the knowing clarity of appearing itself: not a light behind things, not a knower apart, and as empty as everything else. It is not brightness, and it is never missing; there are six clarities, not one lamp, so I say luminous appearances, not one luminous clarity. The error is lifting the sameness out and making it a witness, a mirror or a ground. I AM names luminous clarity itself; the sense of "I" added to it is the error, not the authentic clarity. Anatta freed what it relinquished, the self, but not yet the world; each deconstruction frees only what it has let go of, and even now the taste is still in oscillation. Vivid and not found are one fact, seen twice; spontaneous presence is dependent arising with no producer.

Then the body. The basis is empty of being a ground; the five lights are not a stuff, but the clarity of appearing in five qualities, to be read against the five elements of Indian and Tibetan thought, not modern ideas of light. Hold an apple: a habit ties hardness, red and sweetness into one solid thing. Untie the knot, and nothing is lost: what we call things are only the six lamps, unrecognised, the body included. The rainbow body is that knot untied all the way through, the body included: in essence an actualized realization, carried through waking, dreaming and deep sleep. Where I part: what frees is seeing through the inherent framework, not where the winds go. A vanishing corpse is reported, not proved, and a body of light is not a goal.
