## Nothing Turns Into Light: Luminous Clarity and the Rainbow Body (AA40, 13:42)

### Title

Nothing Turns Into Light: Luminous Clarity and the Rainbow Body | After Anatta

### Description

A passing sound is clear as it comes; nothing had to switch on. An old story tells of a body that shrinks to hair and nails. What could the one have to do with the other? Here I bring together what I have written, across my books and articles, on non-dual luminous clarity and the rainbow body.

Luminosity, for me, is the knowing clarity of appearing itself: not a light behind things, not a knower apart, and as empty as everything else. It is not brightness, and it is never missing; there are six clarities, not one lamp. The error is lifting the sameness out and making it a witness, a mirror or a ground. I AM names luminous clarity itself; the sense of "I" added to it is the error, not the clarity. Anatta freed what it relinquished, the self, but not yet the world; each deconstruction frees only what it has let go of, and even now the taste is still in oscillation. Vivid and not found are one fact, seen twice; spontaneous presence is dependent arising with no producer.

Then the body. The basis is empty of being a ground; the five lights are not a stuff, but the clarity of appearing in five qualities. Hold an apple: a habit ties hardness, red and sweetness into one solid thing. Untie the knot, and nothing is lost. The rainbow body is that knot untied all the way through, the body included: in essence an actualized realization, carried through waking, dreaming and deep sleep. Where I part: what frees is seeing through the inherent framework, not where the winds go. A vanishing corpse is reported, not proved, and a body of light is not a goal.
