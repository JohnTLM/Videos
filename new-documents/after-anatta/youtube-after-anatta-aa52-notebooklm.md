### Title

Rainbow Body in Four Lenses: Why All Four Videos | After Anatta

### Description

A master dies in a small room in eastern Tibet, and over a week, the story goes, the body grows smaller until only hair and nails remain. The rainbow body is easy to inflate into supernatural magic, and just as easy for a modern mind to dismiss. For many years it has been a deep interest of mine. To read it in a balanced way, I looked into four areas and made a video on each, because no single lens is enough on its own.

1. The cultural background of the five elements: Tibet keeps two fives, Chinese and Indian, that meet in history but never merge; in the Indian stream the elements are qualities of what appears, not physical matter. 2. The practices of the body: the habit of taking the world as solid lives in the muscles and the breath; the street between insight and the body runs both ways; settling, never forcing. 3. The traditions in their own words: what Dzogchen, Gelug, Bön and the Kālacakra report, and why the stories remain reported, not shown. 4. The body's own knowing: a felt sense before words, more immediate but not more fundamental, never a deeper floor; the body takes part in realization.

Then the order I would watch them in, and the mistake each one guards against. Together they give a balanced reading: neither dismissing the rainbow body, nor inflating it.
