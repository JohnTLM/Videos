Four Videos on the Rainbow Body: Why All Four | After Anatta

### Description

A body that shrinks to hair and nails: the rainbow body is easy to dismiss, and just as easy to inflate. For many years it has been one of my interests. To read it in a balanced way, I looked into four things and made a video on each. Here I say why I made them, why they belong together, and the order I would watch them in.

1. 5 Elements: Chinese vs Tibet, the background: the five elements as qualities, not stuff; two fives in Tibet. 2. Inner Winds and Subtle Body Practices: somatic practices are important; the street between insight and the body runs both ways; settling, not forcing. 3. Rainbow Body, According to Reports: the traditions in their own words, and reports never checked. 4. The Body's Own Knowing: knowing before words; direct is not the same as right; the body takes part in realization, while a body turning into light remains reported, not shown.
