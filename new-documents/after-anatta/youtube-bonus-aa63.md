## Dependent Arising Section 6 Part 2: No One Turns the Wheel (AA63, 6:18)

### Title

Dependent Arising Section 6 Part 2: No One Turns the Wheel | Thusness

### Description

After twenty-five chapters of refutation, Nagarjuna stops, and simply explains how suffering works.

The twelve links in Nagarjuna's own chapter on them, the one chapter of his Middle Way with no refutation. Emptiness does not stop the world working: a chain whose links had natures of their own could never work and never end. Consciousness "enters" a new life the way a river follows the bed its past has cut, with nothing passing over. A whole mass of suffering, with nothing else in it: no one owns it. The maker of the round is not a person but not knowing. The one who acts and the action are named together, like a magician's figure making another: real as acting, with no one behind it. Karma works because it is empty, and the chain takes hold through elaboration. And a simple act, typing a message: the typist is named with the typing, as fire with fuel.

Section 6, Part 2 of the Dependent Arising series. Previous: Section 6, Part 1, Arising That Does Not Arise. Next: Section 6, Part 3, Elaboration Stops in Emptiness.
