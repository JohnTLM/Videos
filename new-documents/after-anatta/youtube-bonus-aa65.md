Dependent Arising Section 7 Part 1: An Open Heart That Learns | Thusness

### Description

A friend phones with bad news. Before any thought, something in me turns toward them. Then comes the harder part: knowing what would help.

Section 7 turns to compassion, which this series has left almost untouched. Emptiness does not cause compassion, but it takes nothing from it, and it removes the excuses: "it isn't really happening", "there is no one there". Compassion is conventional, and conventional is fully real. It meets suffering where suffering is. It has two halves. The first is openness of the heart: not a feeling made on purpose, but what being dependent already means, once the habit of standing apart loosens. The second is learning: what this person needs, what helps, and what only feels like helping. Then three traditions I respect, Tsongkhapa, Mipham and Dogen, and where I stand with each. Compassion is not a source sending kindness out. It is one of the conditions that meet, and it is empty too.
