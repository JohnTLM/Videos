## One Taste, Many Faces (AA27, 14:50)

### Title

Dependent Arising, Further Viewing: One Taste, Many Faces | Thusness

### Description

I say dependent arising is spontaneous presence. They are not two things. There are three descriptions of one taste: anatta, no one behind it; dependent arising, nothing in it standing on its own; spontaneous presence, nothing missing and nothing making it. Each has its guard. None is higher, none turns into another, and none of them is the taste. Held properly, the view is tasted in every appearing in six ways: vividly appearing yet unfindable, centerless, mutual, seamless, effortless, intimate. They are not states to reach or to keep. Emptiness stays what it always was: a negation and nothing more.

From the taste, I recognise its many faces: a sound, a thought, a flash of anger, a cause and its effect, a stretch of time, a death, washing the dishes. It is much like an AI model that knows a face by its structure, not by one stored photograph. No one is doing the recognising, and nothing stable carries across: same form, not same stuff. Recognition can misfire, and the test is the shape. Every mistake on this path has the same shape: the doer, put back somewhere else. I say where I hold the same as the masters, and where I part. Then try it with a sound nearby.
