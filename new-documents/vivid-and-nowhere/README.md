## Review of the first video

What it got right: innate grasping and the retreat to moments and atoms; three ways of appearing, none privileged;
the bell searched and not found, in the mind too; non-implicative negation; the conditions as empty; appearing
already as dependent arising; designation as the whole status; illusions that work; emptiness does nothing; clarity
with no knower; ka dag, lhun grub and rigpa as the knowing of the basis; recognition not an achievement; the ending.

Where it slipped (source time):

| Time | Line | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| 3:15–3:24 | "The early thinker Vasubandhu theorized the partless atom… the floor of the physical universe" | Wrong: Vasubandhu argued against it |
| 2:38–2:54 | "A point with zero length cannot add up to anything… never amount to a single passing second" | Added mathematics, not in the article, and contestable |
| 8:35–8:44 | "The reality of the cup functions completely through this infinite horizontal dependence" | Dependence as a mechanism, and an infinite chain |
| 9:48, 10:03 | "Zogchen", "Rickpa" | Pronunciation |

Left out: the Abhidharma background, MMK 7 and 7.34, the chariot, "not even nowhere" (MMK 13.8, 22.11), the
Vigrahavyāvartanī, Longchenpa's *Finding Rest in Illusion*, display, self-release, appearance-emptiness, the three
cautions, recognition as not passive, and wonder (*ngo mtshar*).
