## 1. Isn't Energy the Substrate? (ch91, about 3 minutes)

### Title

Isn't Energy the Substrate? Physics and Dependent Arising | Thusness

### Description

Energy is conserved through every change. Is it the modern successor to substance, the one thing that stays while everything else comes and goes?

This short video follows Feynman's own caution about energy, then three facts from physics: energy depends on the frame you measure from, its conservation follows from a symmetry (Noether's theorem), and in an expanding universe it is not even conserved overall. What looks like a substrate turns out to be a bookkeeping quantity, dependently designated. Nothing comes and nothing goes from its own side, and energy still works, fully.

## 2. Dependent Designation and the Taste of Presence (ep90, about 3.5 minutes)

### Title

Dependent Designation and the Taste of Presence | Thusness

### Description

An essay on the logic of dependence, from the chariot to the immediacy of appearing.

The chariot is found neither in its parts nor apart from them: it is designated in dependence. The same logic reaches red, and the seeing of red. Then the subtle turn: the network of conditions itself taken as a ground, abstraction followed by reification. Seen through, there is no gap between the logic and the taste of presence, and even "no gap, no ground" must not become a new ground. With a word from Longchenpa.

## 4. One Flame Lights Another (ch94, about 19 minutes)

### Title

One Flame Lights Another: Karma and Rebirth Without a Traveler | Thusness

### Description

Karma and rebirth without a traveler: how one life conditions the next with nothing carried and nothing cut off, read through the early discourses, Nāgārjuna, Tibet and Zen.

Annihilation is the shadow of eternalism: both need a self that was there from its own side, to be carried on or cut off. One flame lights another; nothing passes over, and nothing is lost. The clear light read as the knowing clarity of appearing, not a light beneath it.

## 5. Insubstantial Appearances (ch93, about 3.5 minutes)

### Title

Insubstantial Appearances: Vivid, Workable and Empty | Thusness

### Description

Two mirror-image mistakes about appearances, and four points that loosen both: things are vivid, workable and empty.

One mistake makes appearances solid, things with their own nature. The other makes them nothing, mere illusion with no consequences. Four points loosen both: appearances are vivid, they work, they depend, and the relations they depend on are as empty as the things they relate. An injury still hurts; nothing in it was ever found from its own side. With Rovelli and Barad.
