The Transition from Anatta to Emptiness. Epilogue: The Synchronization of Experience and Understanding.

When I first began this journey, I believed I was searching for a deeper realization. Years of investigation gradually revealed something entirely different: the realization of anatta had never been incomplete. What remained incomplete was the conceptual framework through which that realization was understood.

The direct realization of anatta dismantles the inherent observer. Doh-gen’s total exertion dismantles the illusion of independent participation. Nah-gar-joo-nah dismantles the final refuge of ontology itself. Each, as this book reads them, completes the work begun by the others; the convergence is an interpretation offered here, not a claim that Doh-gen and Nah-gar-joo-nah taught the same doctrine.

When the requirement for an ontological 'why' is relinquished, ordinary life is fully rehabilitated. Ethics, science and daily human activity are seen functioning perfectly and lightly—not despite emptiness, but as its very display, for dependent arising is empty.

Reality has always been exactly sufficient. Nothing has been added. Nothing has been removed except the demand that reality exist from its own side. The ordinary world has not been transcended; it has been understood.
